About Dr. Escobar

A doctor who understands the whole journey

Dr. Escobar is a Board Certified OB-GYN, originally from Colombia, who completed residency training at Bronx-Lebanon Hospital and Mount Sinai. That background — training in one of the busiest hospital systems in the country — means every patient gets care built on real, hands-on experience, delivered with warmth and patience at every visit.
